Account and Data Deletion Request

Miky Bible University Seminary (MBUS)

Users of the Miky Bible University Seminary app can request deletion of their account and associated personal data using the instructions on this page.

How to request deletion

Send an email to our support team from the email address associated with your account, if you can access it. Use the subject line Account and Data Deletion Request.

Include the following information so we can identify your account:

  • Your full name.
  • The email address used in the app.
  • Your student or registration ID, if applicable.
  • A short statement confirming that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.

Do not include your password or other unnecessary sensitive information. We may ask for reasonable proof of identity before acting on a request.

Email a deletion request

Email: mikybibleuniversity@gmail.com

What may be deleted?

After verifying your request, MBUS will review and delete the account and associated personal data that can lawfully be deleted, subject to applicable legal, security, academic, and institutional recordkeeping requirements.

Information that may need to be retained

Some information, such as official academic records, enrolment history, certificates, records required by law, or information needed to resolve security and fraud issues, may need to be retained for a legitimate period. If some information cannot be deleted, it will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the MBUS Privacy Policy.

How long does the process take?

We will review your request, verify your identity where necessary, and contact you if further information is required. The time needed to complete a request may depend on verification and any applicable record-retention obligations.
