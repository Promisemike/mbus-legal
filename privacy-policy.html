Privacy Policy

Miky Bible University Seminary (MBUS)

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

Miky Bible University Seminary (MBUS) provides this app for admission applications and for admitted students. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what your choices are.

2. Information we collect

Registration form: your name, gender, date of birth, marital status, nationality, country and address, phone or WhatsApp number, email, church and ministry details, education history, the programme you choose, ministry experience, how you heard about us, your next of kin or emergency contact, the list of documents you say you have, and your declaration.
Student account: your Registration ID, your name and contact details, your programme, your admission status, and your password (stored in a protected, non-readable form).
App use: which programme you belong to, so that you only see your own handouts and announcements. We do not use advertising or tracking tools in the app.

3. Why we use it

To review your application, to contact you, to create and manage your student account, to give you access to your study materials and announcements, to keep academic and administrative records, and to keep the app secure.

4. Who can see it

Authorised MBUS administrators and staff who need it for admissions and student support. Each student can only see their own information. We do not sell your information.
We use trusted service providers to run the app: a cloud database and file storage provider (Supabase) and, when enabled, an email delivery service. They process information only to provide these services.

5. How we protect it

Information is sent over secure connections. Access rules in the database allow students to see only their own data, and only administrators can manage applications and students. Passwords are never stored in readable form. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password private.

6. How long we keep it

We keep application and student records for as long as needed for admission, your studies and the school's records, and as required by law. If your application is not successful, we may keep it for a reasonable period for administrative purposes.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, unless we must keep certain records for the school's academic, administrative or legal needs. You can also ask us to close your account. To make a request, write to the contact below with your Registration ID.

8. Children

The app is intended for adult applicants and students. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before you apply.

9. Where information is stored

Your information is stored on secure cloud servers that may be outside your country. We apply the same protections wherever it is stored.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
