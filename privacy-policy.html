1. INTRODUCTION

Miky Bible University Seminary (“the Seminary,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects the privacy of students, applicants, staff, lecturers, graduates, and visitors who use our mobile application, website, and other digital services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and disclose personal information when you access our app, apply for admission, register for courses, participate in academic activities, or communicate with the Seminary.

By using our services, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. Where consent is legally required, we will request it separately.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Depending on the services you use, the Seminary may collect the following information:

2.1 Personal Information

  • Full name.
  • Gender, where required for legitimate administrative purposes.
  • Date of birth, where necessary for admission or student administration.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Residential or mailing address, where needed for communication or document delivery.
  • Country of residence.

2.2 Educational and Academic Information

  • Previous educational qualifications and institutions attended.
  • Programme or course selected.
  • Admission and registration information.
  • Student identification details.
  • Coursework, submitted assignments, research projects, and academic correspondence.
  • Graduation, certificate, and transcript records.

2.3 Ministry and Church Information

Where relevant to a programme or admission process, we may collect the name of your church or ministry, your position or role, and other ministry-related information you voluntarily provide.

2.4 Technical Information

Our app or service providers may collect limited technical information, such as device type, operating system, app version, usage records, and diagnostic information. The information collected depends on the app's actual features and configuration.

We do not intend to collect information that is unrelated to providing and administering our educational services.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We may use personal information to:

  • Process applications and admission requests.
  • Register students for selected programmes and courses.
  • Create and maintain student records.
  • Communicate academic instructions, announcements, and administrative updates.
  • Receive and review coursework and research submissions.
  • Prepare and verify certificates, transcripts, and other official academic documents.
  • Provide student support and respond to enquiries.
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of the app.
  • Meet applicable legal, regulatory, recordkeeping, and institutional obligations.
  • Improve our educational services and user experience.

We will not use personal information for purposes incompatible with the reasons for which it was collected unless permitted by applicable law or otherwise lawfully authorized.

4. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

Where applicable under Nigerian data protection law, the Seminary will process personal information on an appropriate lawful basis. Depending on the circumstances, this may include consent, steps taken at the applicant's request, performance of an agreement, compliance with a legal obligation, or a legitimate interest that does not override the individual's rights.

Where we rely on consent, individuals may withdraw that consent, subject to applicable legal requirements and the consequences explained at the time.

5. STUDENT REGISTRATION AND ACADEMIC RECORDS

Students and applicants are responsible for providing accurate and complete information.

The Seminary may use registration and academic information to verify eligibility, manage enrolment, maintain academic history, issue official documents, and respond to legitimate requests concerning student records.

Academic records may need to be retained after graduation or withdrawal for verification, institutional recordkeeping, legal compliance, or other lawful purposes.

Students should notify the Seminary if their contact information changes or if they identify an error in their records.

6. DISCLOSURE OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

The Seminary does not sell students' personal information to advertisers.

We may disclose relevant information only where reasonably necessary and lawful, including to:

  • Authorized Seminary administrators, lecturers, and staff who need the information for their duties.
  • Technology, hosting, email, learning-management, and other service providers working on our behalf.
  • Relevant authorities where disclosure is required by law or valid legal process.
  • Authorized persons or institutions requesting academic verification, where there is an appropriate lawful basis.
  • The student or another person specifically authorized by the student, subject to identity verification and applicable law.

We expect service providers who handle personal information for the Seminary to protect it appropriately and use it only for authorized purposes.

We will not disclose a student's private academic information publicly without an appropriate lawful basis or authorization.

7. CERTIFICATES, GRADUATION, AND PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENTS

The Seminary may maintain records of admissions, enrolment, graduation, and qualifications for legitimate institutional purposes.

Names, photographs, testimonials, graduation announcements, or other identifiable information will not be published for promotional purposes without an appropriate lawful basis and, where required, the individual's consent.

Students may contact the Seminary to ask about the use of their photographs or personal information in publicity materials.

8. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

The Seminary will take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction.

Depending on the systems used, these measures may include restricted access, account permissions, secure passwords, backups, staff confidentiality obligations, and appropriate security practices.

No electronic system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their login credentials, avoid sharing passwords, and promptly report suspected unauthorized access.

9. DATA RETENTION

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information. For example, official academic records may need to be kept longer than routine enquiries or technical logs.

When information is no longer needed, we will take reasonable steps to delete it, anonymize it, or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable retention obligations.

10. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Because the Seminary may serve students in different countries and may use service providers located outside Nigeria, personal information may be processed or stored in another country.

Where an international transfer occurs, the Seminary will take steps required by applicable law to ensure that the transfer has a lawful basis and that appropriate safeguards are in place.

11. COOKIES AND THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

If our website or app uses cookies, analytics tools, cloud hosting, payment services, video platforms, or other third-party technologies, those services may collect information according to their own privacy policies and settings.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of third-party services they choose to access.

The Seminary is not responsible for the independent privacy practices of third-party websites or services, although we will take reasonable steps when selecting and managing service providers acting on our behalf.

12. CHILDREN AND MINORS

Where applicants or students are under the age of 18, the Seminary will handle their personal information in accordance with applicable law, including any required parental or guardian authorization and additional protections.

Users must provide truthful information about their age when requested for admission or account administration.

13. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information held about them.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally appropriate.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request information about how their personal information is used.
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

Some requests may be limited where the Seminary must retain records for legitimate academic, legal, security, or administrative reasons.

To exercise a privacy right, contact the Seminary using the details in Section 16. We may ask for reasonable proof of identity before responding.

14. PERSONAL DATA BREACHES

If the Seminary becomes aware of a personal data breach affecting information under its responsibility, it will assess the incident and take appropriate steps to contain, investigate, and address it.

Where required by applicable law, the Seminary will notify the relevant authority and affected individuals within the applicable time limits.

15. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

The Seminary may update this Privacy Policy when its services, technology, institutional practices, or legal obligations change.

The latest version will be published through the app or website with an updated effective date. Where required by law, we will notify affected individuals or obtain renewed consent.
